XSBOOT is a bare-metal USB loader for the iPhone XS (A12/T8020).

The software may or may not be extended to support other iPhone models in the future.

XSBOOT is an independent experimental project. Product names are used only
to identify the target hardware. No affiliation, sponsorship, or endorsement
is claimed.

XSBOOT does not exploit or unlock a phone, sign or distribute licensed firmware, or include
Apple firmware.
